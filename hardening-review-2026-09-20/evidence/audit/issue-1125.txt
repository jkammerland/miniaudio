COMMENT skullernet 2026-05-15T21:27:47Z https://github.com/mackron/miniaudio/issues/1125#issuecomment-4463780631
It seems to be related to sound length / device period size ratio. It may also be possible that all sounds are effected, it's just more noticeable with shorter sounds.

E.g. sound length in example above is 3036 frames. If I set `periodSizeInFrames` to 3036 in `ma_engine_config`, sound is played fully. If I set  `periodSizeInFrames` to 3037 sound can't be heard at all.

This also happens with 0.11.25 and -dev branch.
